CULÉ WIRE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Culé Wire is a personal, automated news project run by one individual. This policy describes what data it handles. It is short because the project handles very little.

We do not collect data about you

Culé Wire has no user accounts, no login, no newsletter and no contact form. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party scripts or fonts. Visiting these pages leaves no record with us.

If you read Culé Wire on Telegram, X or TikTok, those platforms collect data about you under their own privacy policies. We have no access to it beyond the aggregate follower and view counts those platforms show any account holder.

Data used to publish

To publish automatically, the project stores credentials for the accounts it posts to: Telegram, X and TikTok. These belong to the operator of Culé Wire, not to any other person.

Specifically, for TikTok the project stores an access token and a refresh token obtained through TikTok's OAuth flow, authorised by the owner of the @cule.wire account. These tokens are held as encrypted secrets in the project's private code repository and are used for one purpose: uploading a video to that same account's drafts, so the operator can review and publish it from the TikTok app.

The project requests only the video.upload permission. It does not read your videos, your profile, your followers or anyone else's.

News sources

The project reads public RSS feeds published by news outlets. No personal data is collected in the process. Summaries are generated using the Anthropic API, which receives the headline and the excerpt published in the feed and nothing else.

Retention

The project keeps a list of the article links it has already published, so it does not repeat itself. That list contains no personal data. Access credentials are kept for as long as the accounts are in use and are revoked when they are not.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about visitors, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, get in touch and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about a story or photograph published by Culé Wire, can be sent to culewire@gmail.com.
